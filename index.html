01 About

I'm a software engineer and machine learning specialist based in Adelaide. For the last several years I've worked inside startups, mainly Insight Via Artificial Intelligence and Woven Optics, as the person who takes a product from an idea to something running in production: the UI, the data design, the backend, the cloud, and the hardware at the edge.

Before that I completed a PhD at The University of Adelaide and the Australian Institute for Machine Learning, researching general and fine-grained video understanding. That's where the computer vision and data science comes from, along with a habit of looking for the answer hidden in the data.

AI is also how I work, not only what I build. I use coding assistants such as Copilot and Claude Code throughout development, and because I've trained and evaluated models myself, I know where they speed things up and where their output needs checking.

I work directly with founders, stakeholders and customers, and I like mentoring small teams while staying hands-on with the product.
